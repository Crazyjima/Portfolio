Where I'm headed, and why.

A working plan, not a promise — short, mid, and long-term goals, the industry I'm aiming at, and the skills I still need to get there.

Timeline

Goals by horizon

Short-term 1–2 years

Land an entry-level full-stack developer role where I can work across both the frontend and backend. Earn a foundational cloud certification (AWS Cloud Practitioner) and get comfortable deploying and monitoring a real production service, not just a class project.

Mid-term 3–5 years

Grow into a mid-to-senior developer role with real ownership over a product area. Build depth in cloud architecture and start mentoring newer developers the way seniors mentored me early on.

Long-term 7–10 years

Lead a small engineering team, or move toward independent consulting, focused on building reliable software for an industry I actually care about rather than chasing whatever's trending.

Direction

Preferred industry

Why

Motivation & values

I want to build things that people can actually rely on — not just ship features, but earn the kind of trust that comes from software behaving the way it's supposed to, every time.

That's part of why fintech and healthcare IT appeal to me over, say, ad-tech: the stakes are visible, and doing the job well means something concrete for the person on the other end of it.
